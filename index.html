About Saggu Steel Industries

Built on Steel. Backed by Four Decades of Trust.

What began as a small workshop in the 1980s has grown into a full-scale manufacturing unit for steel almirahs and air coolers. Every product that leaves our factory carries the same promise we started with — honest steel, careful craftsmanship, and pricing that respects your hard-earned money. Generations of families and businesses across the region have furnished their homes, offices, and factories with products built by our hands.
